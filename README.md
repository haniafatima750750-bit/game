# Yumcook's Game

This is my first game! It's a little paper-plane flying game, and I'd love to
hear what you think. If you have ideas for making it look or play better, feel
free to share them.

## Download and play

**This game is only for Windows computers. It does not run on Android or
iPhone.**

[Download Yumcook's Game](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1JM8to21wdtiF_-Z7KLZj525mz8EMu16y/view?usp=sharing)

Once downloaded, double-click `YumcooksGame.exe` to play. No Python
installation is needed.

## How to play

- Enter your name and choose Normal, Medium, or Hard.
- Tap/click or press **Space** or **Up Arrow** to fly.
- Avoid the pipes and try to beat your best score.

Your scores are saved on your own computer.

Thanks for playing—keep flying, keep smiling, and see how high you can score!
