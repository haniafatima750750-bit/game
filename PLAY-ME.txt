Yumcook's Game
==============

Windows quick start
1. Extract the YumcooksGame-share.zip file.
2. Double-click YumcooksGame.exe to play. No Python installation is needed.
3. Enter your name, then choose Normal, Medium, or Hard.
4. Press Space, Up Arrow, or click to fly the paper plane.

Scores are saved on this computer in your local app-data folder. They are
not sent with the game and are not shared between computers.

If Windows shows a SmartScreen warning for this unsigned game, only run it if
you trust the person who sent it. You can scan the ZIP with your antivirus
before extracting it.
